01 / ABOUT

Software should be
built to last.

Averon Software Foundation brings together ideas, tools and people around a shared interest in better software. We believe useful technology should be carefully designed, openly developed and made available to the people who need it.

Our work is guided by curiosity, practical engineering and a commitment to making the digital world a little better.
